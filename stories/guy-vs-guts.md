**I. Guy**

The man across from him was a wall of black iron, holding a sword that was really just a slab.

Guy had fought swordsmen before: Kisame's shark-skin blade, Zabuza's cleaver, a hundred faceless ninja with a hundred faceless tantō. This was something else. The armor was shaped like a wolf's skull, and the eye slits glowed faintly red.

But the man's face, before the helm closed, had been tired. It wasn't afraid, and it wasn't angry. He looked like someone who had fought every night for so long that he'd forgotten any other way to live.

*Lee would like him*, Guy thought. *He'd want to know how he trains.*

"I'm Might Guy!" he called out, and gave a thumbs up because he always did. "Konoha's Beautiful Green Beast! I'll show you my youth at full power!"

The swordsman said nothing. Guy hadn't really expected him to.

*Gate of Opening.* His muscles flooded with heat. *Gate of Healing. Gate of Life.* His skin went red. *Gate of Pain. Gate of Limit.* The ground under his sandals cracked. *Gate of View.* *Gate of Wonder.*

Green light boiled off him in sheets of steam, and his blood sang.

*Morning Peacock.*

He threw a hundred punches so fast that the friction set them on fire, and the air in front of him burst into a fan of flame. Three of them got through, striking sparks off the black plate, and he felt the armor give.

**II. Guts**

The heat came first, and then the impacts: three of them, each like a battering ram, crushing the plate in against his ribs.

*Fast.* He hadn't even seen the man move. One moment there was a grinning idiot in green spandex, and the next there was a wall of fire coming at him.

The Brand on his neck was quiet. He noticed that the way you notice a dog that should be barking. It was always bleeding, always screaming whenever something from the other side came near. This man wasn't from the other side. He was human, flesh and blood and bowl cut.

Somehow that was worse.

*Let me out*, said the thing that lived in the armor, the black hound with his own face. *Let me out, let me eat him.*

Guts let it out.

The rage went through him like cold water. He swung once, and the man in green caught the flat of the Dragonslayer on his forearm. The bone should have shattered. It didn't. He swung again and hit air, and again, and hit air again. On the fourth swing, the one he dug out of somewhere that should have been empty, the edge finally caught a shoulder and bit in.

That was one cut out of four. He'd killed apostles with less.

**III. Guy**

His body was tearing itself apart. That's what the gates did, and that was the price. Every second at the Seventh Gate, his muscle fibers snapped and knitted and snapped again. He could feel the timer running in his chest.

So he didn't waste a second of it.

He threw a Leaf Hurricane and then a heel drop, eight strikes landing one after another. He drove the man in black back across the stone, and every blow rang off the armor like a temple bell.

Then the sword came through his guard.

He didn't see it. He didn't see it at all. One moment the blade was off to the left, and the next it was through his crossed arms and into his chest. He heard his own ribs go, a sound like green branches snapping.

Guy landed on one knee and coughed blood onto the stone. He grinned.

*Kakashi*, he thought, *you would hate this guy. He doesn't talk either.*

**IV. Guts**

Twenty-three.

He didn't know the number, but his body did. The armor's spikes had gone into his arms to splint the bones the green man had broken. He couldn't feel his left side. His breath came in wet, ragged pulls.

He took a breath anyway, a real one, the kind Godo used to bark at him about at the forge. *Breathe, you damn brat, or you'll drop the hammer.* The breath steadied him, and the world came back into focus.

Casca. He couldn't die here. He hadn't brought her home.

He set his feet.

**V. Guy**

He knocked the man down.

The man got up.

He knocked him down again, a clean strike to the helm, everything behind it. The black armor folded and hit the ground.

It got up.

The third time, Guy stood over him, chest heaving, steam boiling off his skin. He watched the armor *move on its own*. The plates ground against each other, and the wolf's skull lifted. The figure rose to its feet like a puppet on a dead man's strings.

Then it cut him again, a long red line across his stomach.

Guy staggered back. *What is he?*

But he already knew. He'd seen it in Lee at the chūnin exams, broken in the arena sand and still standing. He'd seen it in Neji. The world kept telling some people *stay down*, and they kept answering *no*.

He'd just never seen it in someone so *sad*.

**VI. Guts**

He fell a fourth time, and this time he didn't get up.

He was somewhere dark and warm and quiet. His heart wasn't beating. He knew that the way you know you're dreaming. He could feel the hound pulling at his body, moving his arms. The armor dragged him back up on its own while he floated somewhere behind his own eyes.

*Rest*, something said. *You've done enough.*

It sounded like her. It sounded like Casca, the way she used to sound, before.

*Not yet.*

He woke up.

His heart slammed back into motion like a door kicked in. He came up out of the dark with the Dragonslayer already moving, an upward cut that should have been impossible. It caught the green man across the chest and *lifted him off his feet*.

**VII. Guy**

He was on his back, looking up at the sky, and he was going to die.

Not right now, maybe, but soon. His blood was everywhere and the Seventh Gate was burning through what he had left. Across the stone the black armor was coming for him again, slow and dragging and unstoppable.

His hand went to his chest, to the last gate.

*The Gate of Death.* The Eighth Gate. Night Guy. He had never opened it, never once, because opening it was the end. The power of a god for the price of everything.

It would end this. It would end this man in black, the armor, the sword, all of it, in one kick.

Guy lay there with his hand over his heart and thought about Lee. Lee would want him to come home.

*He doesn't need it*, Guy realized. *I don't need it.* The man in black was already dead. He was being held up by the armor and by something else, something that had nothing left to burn.

Guy took his hand off his chest.

He got up. He was Might Guy, and he got up.

He threw four more strikes, clean and simple. They weren't technique; they were fundamentals, the first things he'd ever taught Lee. On the fourth, the armor stopped moving.

The man inside it stopped too.

**VIII.**

The gates closed slowly, one by one, and the steam thinned to wisps. Guy was standing in a ruined arena with forty-one hit points' worth of life left in him, though he didn't know the number.

He knelt beside the black armor. The wolf's helm had cracked, and through the gap he could see one eye, already closed.

The Brand on his neck had never bled. Guy didn't know what that meant. He wouldn't have understood it if someone had told him.

He put his fist over his heart.

"You were very youthful," he said quietly, to no one who could hear him. "The most youthful I've ever fought."

Then Konoha's Beautiful Green Beast walked out of the arena on his own two feet. That counted as a win, but it didn't feel like one.
