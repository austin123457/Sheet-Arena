Here, Beast's Relentless Rage held five times running, the last at DC 30 (he needed an 18). Guy never opened the Eighth Gate: he only reaches for it while his opponent still has fight left, and Beast kept ending his turns on the edge.

**I. Guy**

He was enormous.

Guy had to tilt his head back to see the face. It was a lion's face: a white mane, a scarred muzzle, and a smile with far too many teeth. The armor was gold and battered. The weapon in its fist was a two-headed thing that the sheet called Draskula, and it looked heavy enough to anchor a ship.

"You are small," the lion said. His voice sounded like a rockslide. "I hope you are not also weak."

Guy beamed and gave him a thumbs up.

"I'm Might Guy! I'm not weak at all!"

He opened six gates and then the seventh, and green light poured off him like a waterfall turned upside down. The stone under him split in a ring, and his hair stood straight up.

The lion's smile got wider.

*Morning Peacock.*

Five strikes went in, and all five landed. Fire burst across the gold armor, singed the white mane, and tore into the flesh underneath. The lion went backward two full steps, which nothing his size should be able to do.

Then the lion *laughed*.

**II. Beast**

*Fire.*

It ran down his chest and over his arms and into the old scars. It was the first real pain he had felt in a very long time, and the little man had done it with his *fists*.

The rage came up through him gladly, red and warm and familiar.

"*You*," Battle Beast said, and pointed Draskula at the little green man. "You are *worthy*."

It had been centuries. He had crossed the stars looking for one opponent worth the name. He'd found Viltrumites who ran, champions who begged, kings who sent armies so they wouldn't have to stand in front of him themselves. Almost no one had ever made him take two steps back.

He swung. The little man turned his blade aside with one forearm, and it only grazed him.

*Good*, Beast thought. *Good, good.*

Then the little man did it again. He landed seven strikes and missed one, fist and heel and knee, each one hitting like a falling star. When it was over, Beast was on one knee with his blood steaming on the stone, and the arena seemed to tilt.

He got up and swung twice. The first cut found the little man's ribs. He put his whole weight behind the second, and it threw the man in green across the arena like a stone off the end of a sling.

The little man skidded, rolled, and came up *smiling*, with blood on his teeth.

Beast had never loved anything as much as that smile.

**III. Guy**

His body was coming apart.

He could feel it in every fiber: the Seventh Gate tearing him down and building him back up, faster than he could heal. The lion's second swing had broken something in his side. Each breath scraped.

*Don't stop. A true master doesn't stop.*

Guy went in again with a Leaf Hurricane, then a rising heel. The lion went down.

The lion got back up, red-eyed, roaring.

Guy hit him again, harder, and the lion went down again. The lion got up again.

*Kakashi*, Guy thought, a little wildly, *I think this one might be worse than you.*

He threw eight more strikes, and the lion was swaying, hanging on by a thread. The light was going out of his eyes. *One more. Just one more.*

Then Draskula came down.

Guy got his arms up, and it didn't matter. The first blow folded him in half. The second sent him through the far wall of the arena, and he lay in the rubble and couldn't feel his legs.

His hand drifted to his chest. *The Eighth Gate. The Gate of Death.*

He could open it. He'd win. He'd be dead by sunset, but he'd win.

He looked across the arena. The lion was barely standing, his mane matted with blood, breathing in great ragged heaves. Guy could see it in his eyes: *one more hit*, the same thought Guy was having.

Opening the last gate on someone who was already finished wouldn't be a win. It would be an execution.

Guy took his hand off his chest and got to his feet. He would do it the way he'd always done it, with youth and hard work and both feet planted.

**IV. Beast**

He fell.

The world went dark, and from somewhere very far off he heard the little man's fists striking his armor. *There*, said the old voice in him, the one that had been waiting since Dornn. *There it is. Your death, at last, at the hands of someone worthy.*

Beast stood up.

He couldn't say how. Something in him simply refused to lie down.

He fell again.

The dark was closer this time and warmer. He could hear his blood in his ears, slowing. *Let go. You've earned it. This is what you wanted.*

He stood up again.

The little man was hitting him so fast he couldn't see it. Fire bloomed across his chest, and the blows came and came and *came*. Beast went down a third time, and this time he *wanted* to stay down. Truly, after centuries, he wanted it. This was the death he had searched the stars for, here at last in a ruined arena, from a man in green spandex who wouldn't stop smiling.

But his body didn't listen. It never had.

He came up out of the dark a final time with the rage roaring through his chest. He was nowhere near close to dying. He was *alive*.

Battle Beast threw back his head and *roared*.

**V. Guy**

He'd never felt anything like it.

It wasn't a jutsu or a genjutsu. It was just a *sound*, the roar of something enormous and old and so very, very alive. It hit him in the chest like a wall, and for one heartbeat his legs simply stopped.

He was afraid. *Might Guy*, afraid. He almost laughed.

That one heartbeat was all the lion needed.

Draskula came down. Guy saw it coming, and his body didn't move.

*Lee*, he thought as the sky turned over, *you'd have liked him.*

**VI. Beast**

The little man lay in the rubble with his eyes closed. He was still breathing, shallow and slow. The green light had gone out of him, and he looked much smaller now.

Battle Beast stood over him. He was bleeding from more places than he could count, and he was the only one still standing.

He had won, again.

He had wanted death, and he'd come closer to it than ever before. He had felt it brush against him five times today, and all five times he'd refused it. The disappointment sat in his chest like a stone.

*Not today*, said the old voice, sadly. *Not even today.*

He looked down at the small man in green. That man had broken his ribs with his bare hands, set his mane on fire and knocked him down *five times*, all without ever going for whatever power was waiting behind that last gate. Beast had seen him reach for it. He'd seen him choose not to.

Beast didn't understand that choice. He wasn't sure he needed to.

He knelt, slowly, his knees cracking, and laid one enormous hand on the little man's chest. He felt it rise and fall.

"Live," Battle Beast said. "Heal. Grow stronger." He smiled, and it was almost gentle. "And when you are ready, little warrior, *come and find me*."

He got up, slung Draskula over his shoulder, and limped out of the arena. It was the best fight of his very long life.
